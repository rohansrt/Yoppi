Learning that reaches beyond the classroom.

YOPPI works with schools and colleges to give students real practice: trying careers, thinking through real situations and preparing for life after campus.

3 experiences are live today. More are in development with partner institutions.

Built around four pillars.

Every YOPPI experience develops at least one of them: the parts of growing up that marks alone don't measure.

  1. Growth

    Learn, develop and become more.

  2. Exposure

    See more. Imagine what's possible.

  3. Connection

    Connect, collaborate and belong.

  4. Wellness

    Feel better. Be well.

Start small. Then grow what works.

Every partnership starts with a conversation, not a catalogue.

  1. 1

    Talk with us

    Tell us about your students and what you would like to change. We listen first.

  2. 2

    Run a pilot

    Try one experience with a class or a batch, with real participation and honest feedback.

  3. 3

    Grow what works

    Keep what students value, extend it across the institution and measure the difference.
